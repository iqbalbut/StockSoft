    FAQ

    Questions security teams ask us

    Do we have to replace our current security tools?

    No. We integrate with leading EDR, SIEM, cloud and identity platforms. If you have gaps, we'll recommend tooling, but you keep ownership of your stack and your data.

    What happens when you find a real threat?

    An analyst confirms it, contains it within the actions you've pre-approved (isolating a host, disabling an account, blocking an indicator) and calls your on-call contact. You get a written incident summary with the timeline and root cause.

    How is MDR priced?

    Per protected endpoint and user per month, with an annual agreement. Incident response hours, onboarding and the monthly ATT&CK review are included. We share a fixed quote after the briefing.

    Where is our data stored?

    Telemetry stays in your region of choice, is encrypted in transit and at rest, and is retained for 90 days by default. Retention can be extended to meet your compliance needs.

    Can you help with an incident if we aren't a customer?

    Yes. Call the incident hotline. We'll start with emergency response terms and can convert to a retainer afterwards.
